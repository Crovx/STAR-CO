## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges austere architectural brutalism with precision high-luxury digital craftsmanship. Built for an elite clientele, the aesthetic projects supreme technological authority, permanence, and restrained confidence. Emotional response should evoke stepping into a monolith of brushed obsidian and raw steel: quiet, exact, uncompromising, and commanding.

The visual style blends:
- **Architectural Brutalism:** Monolithic blocking, razor-sharp 1px dividers, zero organic softness, unapologetic grid dominance.
- **High-End Luxury Minimalism:** Expansive negative space, pure unadulterated grayscale, immaculate typographic hierarchies, and micro-precision alignments.
- **Tactile High-Tech Polish:** Dark smoked-glass transmittances, delicate hairline borders (#2A2A2A to #3D3D3D), and focused platinum halo glows (#E8E8E8) that mimic anodized aerospace metals under directional gallery light.

## Colors

The palette is absolute zero-chroma monochromatic grayscale. No chromatic temperature biases (no warm ambers, cool blues, or slate greens). All color dynamics rely strictly on luminosity contrast and material finish.

### Core Value Allocations
- **Void Canvas (#0A0A0A):** The master ambient backdrop. Represents absolute depth.
- **Tonal Substrates (#111111, #161616):** Recessed layout wells, alternating section backdrops, and navigation panels.
- **Surface Elevation (#1A1A1A, #1E1E1E):** Cards, floating dialogs, and modular blocks.
- **Hairline Precision (#2A2A2A, #2E2E2E, #3D3D3D):** 1px mechanical structural boundaries and crosshairs.
- **Muted Metadata (#888888, #A0A0A0):** Micro labels, timestamps, deactivated state indicators, captions.
- **Structural Text (#AAAAAA, #CCCCCC):** High-readability body copy, secondary narrative blocks.
- **Liquid Steel Accents (#C0C0C0, #E8E8E8):** Metallic badges, active hairline borders, subtle hover reflections, focus halos.
- **Pure Apex Light (#FFFFFF):** Primary typographic statements, hero titles, inverted solid CTA buttons.

## Typography

Typographic execution relies on the pairing between Space Grotesk’s architectural, technical glyphs and Inter’s systematic neutrality.

- **Headlines & Metric Callouts:** Rendered in Space Grotesk. Track titles tight (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to establish solid, sculptural headers with clean optical cohesion.
- **Labels & Micro-Data:** Set in uppercase Space Grotesk with expanded letter spacing (`+0.12em` to `+0.2em`) to mirror technical instrumentation and studio blueprints.
- **Narrative & Body:** Rendered in Inter with regular weights (`400`) and open line-heights (`1.6`–`1.65`) to ensure readability against #0A0A0A and dark gray base surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by a strict 12-column architectural grid with structural continuous 1px rules acting as both visual borders and grid separators.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with 2.5rem gutters and 5rem outer margins. Modules adhere rigidly to column boundaries; asymmetric structural pairings (e.g., 4 col summary vs 8 col display canvas) heighten tension.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 1.5rem gutters and 3rem canvas margin. Multi-column cards compress to 4-column groupings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 1.5rem canvas margin. Columns collapse to 100% width stacked containers separated by structural horizontal hairline rules.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component interiors use concise steps (`space-sm` to `space-lg`), while macro narrative sections use stark gaps (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to establish monumental scale.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth is achieved through glassmorphic smoked-layering and light refraction rather than conventional soft drop-shadows.

1. **Base Ground (Level 0):** Pure `#0A0A0A`, absolute flat black.
2. **Structural Slabs (Level 1):** Solid `#111111` or `#161616` bounded by 1px solid `#2A2A2A` hairline borders.
3. **Floating Glassmorphic Paneling (Level 2):** Semi-transparent `#141414E6` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and 1px border `#3D3D3D`.
4. **Active Platinum Halo (Focus / Hover):** Elevated cards or active modal edges adopt a 1px border of `#E8E8E8` accompanied by a localized ambient halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(232, 232, 232, 0.12), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is strictly **Sharp (0)**. 
- All buttons, panels, dialogs, inputs, and modal canvases employ `border-radius: 0px`. 
- Corners are surgically perpendicular, referencing high-end industrial design, brutalist monolithic architecture, and machined slate. 
- Rounded geometry is strictly reserved for native system toggles or circular status indicators (which remain pure geometric ellipses without rounded card parent containers).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0A0A0A`, 0px radius, font Space Grotesk `label-lg`, uppercase. Hover: background `#E8E8E8`, subtle outer glow `0 0 16px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary (Technical Outline):** Background transparent, 1px border `#3D3D3D`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: border `#E8E8E8`, background `#1A1A1A`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Ghost Action:** Background transparent, text `#AAAAAA`, hover text `#FFFFFF` with horizontal 1px underline growing from 0% to 100% width.

### Cards & Modular Slabs
- Structural box model with 0px radius, `#1A1A1A` background, 1px border `#2A2A2A`.
- Padding: `space-xl`.
- Interactive cards transition to `#1E1E1E` with 1px `#E8E8E8` border and the platinum halo glow on hover.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Surface: `#111111`, 1px border `#2E2E2E`, 0px radius, text `#FFFFFF`, typography `body-md`.
- Placeholder: `#888888`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#FFFFFF` with micro halo `0 0 12px rgba(232, 232, 232, 0.15)`. No default browser rings.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp 16px x 16px squares (checkbox) or strict circular bounds (radio).
- Unchecked: 1px border `#3D3D3D`, background `#0A0A0A`.
- Checked: Background `#FFFFFF`, inner indicator `#0A0A0A`.

### Badges & Technical Chips
- Background: `#161616`, 1px border `#2E2E2E`, text `#A0A0A0`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Space Grotesk `label-sm` uppercase.
- Accent Variant: 1px border `#E8E8E8`, text `#FFFFFF`, background `#1E1E1E`.

### Lists & Data Grids
- Clean tabular rows separated by 1px solid `#2A2A2A` rules.
- Row hover: `#141414` background tint across entire row block.
- Headers: Space Grotesk `label-md` in `#888888`.
- Cells: Inter `body-md` in `#CCCCCC`.

### Technical Crosshairs & Studio Watermarks
- Decorative interface elements: 1px crosshair marks (`+`) positioned at the intersections of major grid cells using `#3D3D3D`.
